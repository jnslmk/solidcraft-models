**Checked 2026-10-25.** Scope: standard solid-wall, open-top boxes, excluding perforated/ESD/coupling-system and hinged-lid/case variants. Dimensions below are manufacturer-listed; they are planning data, not a fit guarantee.

## Drawing, geometry limits and cupboard use

All six linked pages offer AUER's [hinge-hole template PDF](https://www.auer-packaging.com/pdf/drawings/eg_43_hole.pdf), titled “Lochbild Eurobehälter EG43”, dated 2017-05-05, material PP. It is **not** a full container drawing: it does not supply the support lip, base underside/ribs, wall draft, corner radii or stacking interface needed for a close-fitting cupboard. Its title block calls out **general tolerances DIN 16742-TG3**, but the product pages do not publish numeric overall-envelope tolerances; do not turn that template callout into an assumed ±mm allowance without the applicable standard and AUER confirmation. The listed 370 × 270 mm internal dimensions and container base design do not describe the exact mouth opening or bottom bearing footprint: those remain unknown, so do not infer them by subtracting wall thickness. No item-specific 3D model or complete container drawing was listed on the inspected first-party product pages.

AUER says the family is designed for stacking and conveying, but publishes no numeric stack pitch/load rating in these pages. Usable internal height in stack is the published clear-height value, not a stacking pitch. Do not infer total stacked height by adding nominal external heights: the engagement/overlap is undocumented. For a close-fitting cupboard, treat nominal 400 × 300 mm external envelope as initial sizing only, add practical clearance, and measure the delivered selected variant before finalizing shelf stops/fit. Published nominal dimensions are not a guarantee of fit.

### Initial cupboard modeling decisions

- Use the full **400 × 300 mm external envelope**, not the internal dimensions, for each bay. Keep orientation selectable: 300 mm frontage × 400 mm depth or 400 mm frontage × 300 mm depth. Allow separately for carcass panels, door/hinge intrusion, stops and any drawer hardware.
- Keep side clearance, rear clearance and overhead handling clearance as independent design parameters, not assumed AUER tolerances. For shelf-supported boxes, clear bay height is box external height plus handling clearance; vertical shelf pitch additionally includes shelf/support thickness. Stacked interior clear height is not the value to use for this.
- **[INFERENCE / design recommendation]** Start with bottom-supporting shelves or trays rather than rails that hang a loaded box from its rim. Rim support requires measurements and load evidence not supplied by the catalogue. AUER's [without-drawer system trolley](https://www.auer-packaging.com/en/System-trolley-for-Euro-containers-without-drawers.html) also describes containers secured on edged trays; its listed systems are not a dimensional template for this 400 × 300 mm cupboard.
- Before cutting parts or designing contact rails, measure a real selected box: maximum envelope including ribs/handles, underside bearing footprint and feet, rim underside/overhang, corner radii, withdrawal/lift space, and deformation under the intended load. Confirm lid choice and actual load separately.

**Current CAD decision (2026-10-25):** use the [independently constructed native SolidCraft layout model](../models/auer_euroboxes/README.md), not the restricted GrabCAD solids. It covers all six nominal heights with editable parameters and explicitly assumed foot/bearing/rim details. Its internal height deliberately differs from catalogue values; it is not manufacturing or certified contact geometry. The [earlier CAD availability research](auer-eurobox-cad-availability.md) remains useful for provenance and licensing context, but its reuse-first recommendation has been superseded because of redistribution restrictions.

## Variants and lids

- The standard product pages expose choices for front and long-side handles; the 400 × 300 × 75 page is code `EG 43/75 HG` with closed handles both sides, while the other 400 × 300 sizes' listed default configurations have open front handles and closed long-side handles, except `EG 43/17 HG`, listed with both handles closed. Smooth closed base is the listed standard choice; the 400 × 300 category row additionally offers a reinforced closed base option for several heights. Recheck the option selected on the exact order configuration.
- AUER separately offers [hinged-lid Euro containers](https://www.auer-packaging.com/en/Euro-containers-with-hinge-lid.html), [Pro hinged-lid containers](https://www.auer-packaging.com/en/Euro-containers-with-Pro-hinged-lid.html) and [place-on lids](https://www.auer-packaging.com/en/Euro-containers.html). Its category description says hinged lids protect contents and permit stacking; Pro lids add clamping/sealing features. The standard-box pages link to place-on lid `DE 43`. These sources do not state item-specific lid height, combined external height, lid mass, or stacking pitch; obtain those details if the cupboard must accommodate a fitted lid.
- Do not treat perforated, ESD, coupling-system, hinged-lid or case products as geometrically interchangeable standard solid boxes. AUER describes these as separate families in the [Euro-container overview](https://www.auer-packaging.com/en/Euro-containers.html).
