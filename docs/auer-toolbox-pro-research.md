# AUER Toolbox Pro — two smallest sizes

**Checked 2026-10-29.** Scope: official AUER catalogue dimensions and visual references for independent editable layout models; no third-party CAD was consulted or traced. No physical sample was measured. Manufacturer-listed dimensions below are nominal published values, **not measurements made in this project or a fit guarantee**.

## Confirmed sizes and product codes

**Yes: 400 × 300 × 120 mm and 400 × 300 × 175 mm are the two smallest Toolbox Pro sizes.** The [official Pro family table][family] starts with these two, followed by 400 × 300 × 230, 340 and 450 mm; the 600 × 400 mm footprint starts at 175 mm high. Do not substitute the standard Eurobox's 170 mm height for the second Toolbox Pro's **175 mm**.

All dimensions in this table are converted exactly from AUER's centimetres to millimetres. AUER labels the horizontal interior-related value **“Inlet”**, not a full interior bounding box.

| Size / official product pages | Exterior L × W × H | Published inlet | Published usable interior height | Published stacking height | Material | Empty weight |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| Smallest: [TB 4311 F1][small-f1] / [TB 4311 F4][small-f4] | 400 × 300 × 120 mm | 394 × 248 mm | 77 mm | 110 mm | ABS | 1.64 kg |
| Second smallest: [TB 4316 F1][tall-f1] / [TB 4316 F4][tall-f4] | 400 × 300 × 175 mm | 394 × 248 mm | 132 mm | 165 mm | ABS | 1.87 kg |

- **F1:** dark-grey box, dark-grey lid; **F4:** dark-grey box, light-grey lid. The four linked item pages explicitly identify those colour combinations and show the same size-specific technical values.
- All four checked item pages show **“without lock”**. The family configurator offers a one-lock option for these sizes; do not confuse this optional keyed security lock with the two normal lid-closure catches.
- `4311` and `4316` are article identifiers, not literal exterior heights of 110 and 160 mm. Use the separately published external heights above.
- The published inlet and usable height can be recorded as **394 × 248 mm opening, 77 / 132 mm usable height**, but **must not be advertised as a guaranteed rectangular interior volume**. Bottom floor footprint, wall draft, local intrusions and interior corner radii are not dimensioned in these product tables.
- **Derived, not independently measured:** exterior height minus stacking height is 10 mm for both sizes. Treat the listed stacking height as the nominal incremental height of an additional stacked box; two 120 mm units would therefore nominally total 230 mm, and two 175 mm units 340 mm. The 10 mm difference is **not** a published foot height, lug thickness or required shelf clearance.

## Appearance and operation

The observations in the following table come from the official photographs and linked official videos, not from dimensioned drawings. Shape descriptions are visually supported; **all feature sizes, positions, clearances, radii and detailed profiles remain approximations if modelled**. The family video demonstrates the Pro interface across the range; its red demonstration box is not evidence of either selected size's exact local dimensions.

| Feature | Official evidence / observed appearance | Limits for independent modelling |
|---|---|---|
| Main body | Both sizes have a dark, broadly rectangular, smooth-sided body with relatively small rounded outer corners. The front has a broad shallow recessed panel between the two front corner towers, with an angular lower relief/grip area. The 175 mm body extends the side walls; the lid and front-corner mechanism have a closely similar appearance. See the four F4 item photos below. | No body corner radius, wall thickness, draft angle or recess depth is published. Do not copy standard Eurobox ribs: these are different products. |
| Lid | Light grey on F4; dark grey on F1. A raised rectangular perimeter surrounds a lower central top panel. There are elongated recessed receiving areas near the corners, visibly associated with stacking. The open photos show two rear hinge locations, extensive underside stiffening ribs, mounting bosses and a central round boss near the front inside edge. [AUER explicitly states][family] **spring-loaded lid** and **automatic lid locking**. | Overall exterior height includes the closed lid; no separate lid thickness, spring dimensions, hinge-pin diameter or opening angle is published. A simple plain slab would miss the characteristic perimeter/central-panel appearance. |
| Normal lid catches / latches | Two dark catches sit at the upper front corners immediately under the lid, wrapping toward the sides. The small light-grey inset on each outer side is the release control. The [live demonstration][video-live] around 5–7 s shows the user's hand at the corner control and the lid opening; the open photos show raised catch tongues. | These are not two large coloured toggle clasps on the front face. Exact pivot, spring and engagement geometry are not dimensioned. Do not infer reliable working catches from a visual model. |
| Carry handle | The lid has a wide, thin **folding U-shaped top carry handle**, stowed flat within the top recess, rather than a permanently protruding central loop. The [animation][video-animation] at 22 s shows it raised above a linked stack. | Raised handle envelope and pivot position are not published. A folded handle should remain within the nominal closed-box height for a layout reference; raised-handle clearance requires measurement. |
| Optional front / side handles | AUER separately sells [optional handles][handles]: `TB OVG` is “Optional handle on the front”; `TB OSG` is “Optional handle on the side” (official category table). The family banner shows some boxes fitted with extra handles. | Do not assume a separately attached front or side handle is supplied with a default selected box. The product pages warn **“Image similar”** and **“The photograph includes optional accessories.”** Keep accessory choices distinct from the standard lid handle. |
| Feet, underside and stacking interface | The [animation][video-animation] at 15 s exposes an underside reinforced by a dense grid of ribs, plus protruding, hooked/stepped peripheral engagement lugs. At 14–18 s those features approach and engage the matching lid recesses. These are not four tall rubber feet. The selected-size closed photos show only a low bottom reveal; they do not expose the complete bearing surfaces. | No foot/lug dimensions, count-specific drawing, bearing footprint or base-rib depth was found in the checked item/family sources. Any pad count and position in a layout model is an explicit visual approximation. Do not use it to design tightly fitting mating hardware or assume a full flat perimeter bears on shelf runners. |
| Linked stacks | [AUER describes][family] a **patented bayonet fitting** for fast, secure linking; its [toolbox overview][overview] says the fittings also secure boxes to suitable transport/hand trolleys. The animation shows linked boxes carried as a unit. This is a mechanically coupled stack, not just an open Eurobox rim stack. | The catalogue's stacking height can size nominal stacks. Exact bayonet travel, locking action, load rating and compatibility with any independently made mating part are not established by the pictures. |

**Orientation for visual models:** the illustrated front with the two catches is the long face, approximately the 400 mm direction; the hinge is opposite it. This orientation is inferred from the images, not a separately dimensioned front-view drawing. A cupboard may rotate the box to 300 mm frontage × 400 mm depth, but that puts the front controls on a side: do not silently rotate them to the narrow face.

## Published geometry versus approximations

Use the numeric table as the published planning envelope. Maintain a separate list of visual/design assumptions for everything else: lid section thickness, shell wall/base thickness, internal corner radii and draft, top handle section and pivots, catch sizes, hinge shape, corner receiving recesses, underside feet/lugs, floor grid and front reliefs. Neither photographic pixel estimates nor nominal 10 mm stacking overlap turn those into measured geometry.

The checked item pages link **optional-handle drilling-pattern PDFs**, not a complete dimensioned box/interface drawing: `tb_43_osg_hole.pdf` and `tb_43_ovg_hole.pdf`. Their links do not establish the unlisted bearing or bayonet dimensions. No numeric exterior tolerance, shelf-support load capacity or exact usable floor rectangle is given by the checked product tables.

For a cupboard, allow clearance around the **400 × 300 mm exterior** and the **120 / 175 mm closed heights**, not around the inlet. Individually supported levels require the full closed height plus deliberate handling clearance; do not use the shorter stacking height unless boxes actually engage one another. Measure real underside contact surfaces and the intended load before treating modelled feet/ribs as structural support data.
